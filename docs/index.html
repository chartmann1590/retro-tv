Everything You Love About Cable

Engineered for the Ultimate Nostalgia

📺

Always-Live Playback

Every show and movie has a deterministic start and end time. Change channels or reload the page and you drop right into the current scene—no per-user pause or rewind, just real television.

🎬

Retroflix Video On Demand

A full Netflix-style interactive On Demand interface (`/vod`). Search your library, explore featured spotlights, filter by genre or recent additions, and launch straight to your HDMI TV.

🏈

Live Sports & Gameplay Field Radar

Dedicated sports channel & interactive game center (`/sports`). Live scores, neural TTS announcer play-by-play, and dynamic SVG gameplay radars: football 100-yd gridiron with down/distance, baseball diamond with active base runners, basketball court, hockey rink, and soccer pitch.

⛅

Local Weather & Live Doppler Radar

Generates an authentic 90s-style Weather Channel loop with real National Weather Service forecasts, hourly trajectories, 7-day outlooks, live radar sweeps, and natural neural voice narration every 30 minutes.

📰

Local News Wire Channel

Automatically fetches your local RSS news feed and pictures, composing broadcast headline slides with neural voice narration and commercial breaks on-the-fly.

📱

Phone Remote & USB Remote Control

Control everything from your phone browser (`/remote`) or a 2.4GHz wireless USB remote. Dedicated SPORTS and ON DEMAND buttons, amber LCD channel display, D-pad navigation, and instant volume control.

🎞️

Fair Commercial Rotation

Commercial compilations are intelligently sliced and tracked so you never see the same commercials back-to-back, rotating evenly across program breaks.

🏷️

Automatic Genre Movie Channels

Auto-creates dedicated movie channels (Action, Comedy, Sci-Fi, Horror) when your library has 5+ qualifying movies, keeping three full days of scheduled lineups generated in advance.

🌐

Watch In Any Browser

Stream your live cable channels anywhere on your local network at `/watch`, complete with on-the-fly audio transcoding and HLS remuxing for mobile and desktop browsers.

🤖

100% native Kotlin companion app with UDP broadcast auto-discovery, 6-digit Web UI pairing, ExoPlayer Media3 live TV streaming, interactive EPG guide, Retroflix VOD, and tactile remote.

Get Up & Running in Minutes

Simple Setup on Any Linux Box

bash — retro-tv@raspberrypi:~ 
# 1. Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/chartmann1590/retro-tv.git
cd retro-tv

# 2. Set up Python virtual environment
python3 -m venv venv
venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt

# 3. Configure your sports backend (optional: connects to ArenaPulse)
cp .env.example .env
# Edit .env with ARENAPULSE_URL=http://<your-sports-server>:3000

# 4. Start Retro-TV!
venv/bin/python app.py
# Opens on http://localhost:5000 with scanning & schedules auto-initialized
